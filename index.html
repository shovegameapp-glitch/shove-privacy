Shove — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Shove does not collect, store or share any personal data.

Data collected

None. The app has no accounts, sign-up, ads, analytics or third-party tracking, and it does not use an internet connection.

Stored on your device

Your settings (sound, vibration, language), game statistics and solved puzzles are stored only on your device. They are never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

Permissions

The app uses the vibration permission only for short haptic feedback on taps.

Children

The app collects no data from users of any age.
